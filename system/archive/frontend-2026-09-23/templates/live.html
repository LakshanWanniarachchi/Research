The operating point sets the threshold only. high_recall (0.2736) is tuned to miss as few infarctions as possible and therefore raises many false alarms; on the test fold it flagged about a third of healthy recordings. default (0.5) is the balanced point. The model was trained on clinical PTB-XL recordings, not on AD8232 signals, so live results carry that difference on top.
