{{ icon('i-pulse', 20) }} ECG Screening System
Single-lead myocardial infarction screening

Deep-learning screening from a single ECG lead.

A 1D convolutional ensemble reads ten seconds of Lead I and reports a probability of a myocardial infarction pattern. Signals arrive from an AD8232 front end on an ESP32, over Wi-Fi or USB.

  • {{ icon('i-wifi') }}ECG streamed from the device and scored as it arrives
  • {{ icon('i-shield') }}Each account sees only the recordings from its own devices
  • {{ icon('i-flask') }}Model trained on the PTB-XL clinical dataset, never on live signals
